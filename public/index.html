Site blocking the audit? Paste your page's HTML instead

Some websites, for example those behind Cloudflare protection, refuse automated visitors. You can paste the page source here and we will audit it directly.

  1. Open your page in the browser.
  2. Right-click and choose View page source (or press Ctrl+U, Cmd+Option+U on Mac).
  3. Select everything, copy it and paste it below.

Tip: also type the page address in the box above. It lets us check links, robots.txt and the sitemap. Your HTML is analyzed once and is not stored.
